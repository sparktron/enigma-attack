## Neither branch: when to use neither draft

- **The preflight or a positive control fails at the rerun**, including the
  windowed control (fewer than 3 of 12): the result is withheld, as the v3
  decision in the windowed-climb entry requires. Record the failure and fix
  the gate. Do not report the sweep as null or positive.
- **A candidate confirms at only one companion, or sits between z 4.6 (the
  calibration's null maximum) and 6 at both:** this is branch (a). The
  threshold was not tuned on these results and is not tuned now. Name the
  candidate in the interpretation as the closest miss.
- **No candidate confirms but `{{TOP_Z}}` is above 7.0:** this is branch (a),
  with the prediction `null-top-near-expected-maximum` refuted. Check the top
  candidate by hand (its plaintext and its plugboard against v2's) and record
  what it is. Without companion confirmation it is not a find.

## Branch (a): null at stated power

### Draft history entry

```markdown
## phase1-body-direct-sweep-v3

Status: completed {{END_DATE}}; hypothesis **not supported**: no retained
candidate is companion-confirmed.

Hypothesis: as v2, with BYQMZ's key one the whole-message climb could miss,
most plausibly because one letter of BYQMZ was dropped or inserted. A complete
middle-past-notch sweep of BYQMZ with the head-and-tail windowed climb
(W = 117) then retains a candidate that the companion confirmation accepts:
FKQLZ and XFEDT both at best-start z ≥ 6.

Refuted by: no retained candidate companion-confirmed. The power was stated in
advance: 0.78 [0.67, 0.85] clean and 0.54 [0.43, 0.64] with one indel. Given
v2's null, the relevant figure is conditional: 0.29 [0.18, 0.43] for a
single-indel key the whole-message climb missed, and 3 of 10 for a clean one.

Configuration:
[experiments/phase1-body-direct-sweep-v3/config.json](../experiments/phase1-body-direct-sweep-v3/config.json),
committed in `6b6ad2f` before the run. Raw result:
[artifacts/phase1-body-direct-sweep-v3.json](../artifacts/phase1-body-direct-sweep-v3.json).
It is long-running and not regenerated by the drift check.

Run record: chunks computed at `{{CHUNK_COMMIT}}` with the batched engine and
10 workers on an i9-10900K, from {{START_UTC}} to {{END_UTC}}:
{{RUNTIME_H}} h for 27,418,560 settings ({{MS_PER_SETTING}} ms per setting,
against a projected 19 to 26 h). The runner was then rerun at
`{{CONTROLS_COMMIT}}` (dirty: {{CONTROLS_DIRTY}}), which carries the
windowed-control gate. It re-ran the preflight ({{PREFLIGHT_PASSED}}) and
every control ({{CONTROLS_PASSED}}; windowed control
{{WINDOWED_CONTROL_RECOVERED}} of 12 recovered exactly, at least 3 required),
resumed {{CHUNKS_RESUMED}} of 1,560 chunks from the checkpoint, and confirmed
the candidates.

### Observed

- Coverage: 100% of the middle-past-notch reducible space, the same space as
  v2, which holds an exact equivalent of 71.6% of daily keys at 167 letters.
- Windowed score distribution over every setting: mean {{MEAN}}, sd {{SD}} per
  letter. Maximum {{MAX}} (z = {{TOP_Z}}); rank 2 {{RANK2_SCORE}}.
- Companion confirmation over the 100 retained candidates: none confirmed.
  The best minimum over FKQLZ and XFEDT is z = {{BEST_MIN_Z}} (rank
  {{BEST_MIN_Z_RANK}}), against the calibration's null maximum of 4.60.
- Indicator confirmation: {{IND_COMPATIBLE}} of 100 candidates have a
  compatible ring setting; the best pooled date score is {{IND_BEST}}.
- {{V2_OVERLAP}} of the 100 retained plugboards were also retained by v2.

Predictions: the deciding prediction failed (no confirmed candidate). The two
conditional on a confirmation do not apply. The top score at the noise
maximum (z between 5.0 and 7.0): {{NULL_TOP_HELD_OR_REFUTED}}.

### Interpretation (inference)

- Given v2's null, this null multiplies the odds of a standard Enigma I
  reading with a once-garbled BYQMZ by about 0.71: the planted draws give
  0.29 [0.18, 0.43] for the conditional detection, so 0.57 to 0.82. That is a
  modest test, as preregistered, not a decisive one.
- Taken together, on the same planted draws (post hoc, from the two
  exploratory joint artifacts), v2 or v3 detects and confirms 46 of 80
  single-indel keys (0.575) and 73 of 80 clean keys (0.91). Both nulls
  together therefore multiply the odds of a once-garbled standard reading by
  about 0.43, and of an ungarbled one by about 0.09.
- What is left of the standard-Enigma hypothesis now sits mainly in four
  places, none of which v3 tested: an indel in the middle third of BYQMZ
  (letters 50 to 117, 8 of 32 windowed and 2 of 32 whole); the 28% of keys
  with no exact equivalent under the middle-past-notch rule; two or more
  faults; and machines or procedures outside the declared assumptions (other
  reflectors, Naval wheels, other indicator conventions, non-Enigma).
- This does not show the date is non-Enigma.

### Decision

- `accepted_break` stays false.
- The crib-free route at this space and rule is exhausted for one indel. The
  next steps, cheapest first: the FKQLZ length calibration
  (phase1-fkqlz-length-calibration-v1, already preregistered), which decides
  whether FKQLZ can carry a sweep of its own; the middle-complete rule (about
  nine times the cost) for keys with no exact equivalent here, whose power
  must be measured before it runs; and the Bombe only if phase2-crib-prior-v1
  passes its gates on held-out traffic, which as of 2026-10-04 it cannot.
```

```markdown
Status: completed {{END_DATE}}; deciding prediction **held**: a retained

### Observed

- Confirmed candidate(s) at rank(s) {{CONFIRMED_RANKS}}. For the best:
  wheel order {{CAND_ROTORS}}, rings as swept {{CAND_RINGS}}, start
  {{CAND_START}}, plugboard {{CAND_PLUGBOARD}} ({{CAND_PAIRS}} pairs), window
  {{CAND_WINDOW}} (head or tail), sweep score {{CAND_SCORE}} (z

- Companion z: FKQLZ {{CAND_Z_FKQLZ}} at start {{CAND_START_FKQLZ}}; XFEDT

  null maximum 4.60.
- Indicator confirmation: compatible ring setting {{CAND_IND_COMPATIBLE}};
  pooled date score {{CAND_IND_SCORE}}; message keys {{CAND_MESSAGE_KEYS}}.
- Decrypt, raw and unedited (first 60 letters of each):
  BYQMZ `{{CAND_PT_BYQMZ}}`, FKQLZ `{{CAND_PT_FKQLZ}}`, XFEDT
  `{{CAND_PT_XFEDT}}`.
- Where the window choice implies an indel: the letter at which the head and
  tail settings part, {{CAND_INDEL_POSITION}}.
- Score distribution: mean {{MEAN}}, sd {{SD}}, maximum {{MAX}} (z
  {{TOP_Z}}).

Predictions: deciding prediction held. Rank one: {{RANK_ONE_HELD}}. Plugboard

### Interpretation (inference)

- Companion confirmation at z ≥ 6 at both companions was calibrated on 240
  nulls, whose maximum was 4.60, and none reached 6. A confirmed candidate is
  therefore strong evidence for a shared key under the declared model. It is
  not yet a break, because the calibration's nulls were random keys and the
  v1 slice, and nothing in it tested a structured near-miss.
- A window-only detection means BYQMZ most likely carries a fault near
  letter {{CAND_INDEL_POSITION}}. That has to be shown on the ciphertext, not
  assumed: §7.1 requires a minimal source-error ledger.
- If the indicator check disagrees (no compatible ring setting, or keys that
  do not decrypt the indicators), the body and the indicator procedure
  disagree. That points at the declared indicator convention, not the body
  key, and it is recorded as open under §7.2. It is not explained away.

### Decision: route to ATTACK_PLAN §7

The candidate is accepted only when each item below is done and recorded,
in this order:

1. §7.1: re-encrypt the full key against the observed ciphertext of all
   three messages. List every mismatch, including BYQMZ's `?` at letter 29 and
   any indel the window implies, in a source-error ledger, each entry
   justified independently of the score.
2. §7.2: derive the message keys from the indicators under the 1940+
   clear-Grundstellung procedure, in both orderings, with recovered rings.
   State the body boundaries.
3. §7.3: raw plaintext of all three messages over their full length. A
   German reader judges coherence with no score-driven edits.
4. §7.4: one daily key decrypts all three; FKQLZ and XFEDT each decrypt in
   full, not just at the companion check's best start.
5. §7.5: an independent implementation (not `enigma.py` or
   `enigma_fast.py`) reproduces every decrypt from the recorded key, and
   CryptoCellar's message forms are checked for corroborating metadata.

`accepted_break` is set true only by a separate commit that records all five.
No external announcement is made from this entry.
```
